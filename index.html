About the Artist

My name is Pracci Pro. Real name Bupe.

I'm an artist from Kafue Town, Lusaka, Zambia. It's a small place, but the dreams are big. Hip-Hop/Rap is the language I speak, and WhiteBlood Music is the home.

My latest body of work DONT BELONG HERE!! tells the story of how my life has been for the last 4 years. Every track carries real feelings — the struggles, the wins, the nights that shaped me.

Born and raised in Zambia. Currently chasing the dream in Dehradun, India. The game was missing someone — I'm that guy.
